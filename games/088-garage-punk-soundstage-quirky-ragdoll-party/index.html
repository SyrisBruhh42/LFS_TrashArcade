How to Play

1. The Goal & Lore

Outscore your rival before the 90-second match clock expires (or reach 7 points first) by timing jumps, directional kicks/smashes, and powerup shots to advance through the Championship Bracket.

2. How the Loop Works & Why

Time your jump to intercept high lobs or block low line-drives. Strike the ball with directional spin or recoil to curve shots past the rival keeper. Strike mid-air powerup icons to trigger bonuses.

3. Controls

Desktop: Left/Right Arrows or A/D to move. Up Arrow or W to jump. Space to Smash/Kick (Mid-Flight Boost).

Mobile: Use the on-screen left/right buttons, Jump button, and Smash button.
