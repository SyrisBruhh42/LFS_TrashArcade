How to Play

Movement: You can only move by shooting! The recoil pushes you in the opposite direction.

Rhythm: A beat plays in the background. Shoot ON THE BEAT to gain multiplier combos and jump further.

Goal: Survive on the floating islands, destroy enemies, and don't fall off.

Desktop: WASD / Arrows to aim, Space / Click to shoot.

Mobile: Use the on-screen joystick to aim and the shoot button.
