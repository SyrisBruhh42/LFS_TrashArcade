How to Play

1. The Goal & Lore

You have 30 days before the Grand Starlight Festival to balance your daily schedule, build your personal stats, navigate rival drama, and win the heart of one of 5 romanceable leads—or claim solo superstardom.

2. How the Loop Works & Why

Spend Stamina on Work, Study, Studio Practice, or Shopping to boost Charm, Wit, Talent, and Cash. Visit locations to trigger branching dialogue, combine clues/gadgets, and give favorite gifts to raise Affection.

3. Desktop & Touch Controls

Desktop: Use Mouse to click choices and schedule activities. Press Enter to start or advance text. Press Esc to pause.
Mobile: Tap choices and schedule buttons directly.
