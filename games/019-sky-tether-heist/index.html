How to Play

Goal: Steal loot from the floating islands and return to extraction pads to score.

The Catch: Each collected loot adds a segment to your tail. Your tail can be spotted by guards!

Controls: WASD/Arrows to move. Virtual joystick on mobile. Space/Action button to extract or interact.

Tips: Time your movements carefully. Bank your loot often to shorten your tail and reduce risk.
