How to Play

Desktop:
- WASD/Arrows: Move horizontally.
- Mouse Click & Hold: Fire grappling hook towards cursor and reel in.

Mobile:
- Left Joystick: Move horizontally.
- Right Joystick / Touch area: Aim and fire grappling hook.

MECHANIC: TIME DILATION
When you release ALL controls (no movement keys, no grappling), time slows to a crawl! Use this to perfectly aim your next grapple or plan your route through falling obstacles.

Gain points by swinging high and collecting neon orbs. Don't fall below the destruction laser!
