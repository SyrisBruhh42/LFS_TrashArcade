HOW TO PLAY

Recoil is Movement!

You cannot move normally. You must shoot to push yourself backwards through the steampunk foundry.

  • Mouse/Keyboard: WASD/Arrows or Mouse to aim. Click or Space to shoot/move.
  • Touch: Left stick aims, right area shoots.

Bounce off walls to gain combo multipliers. Smash enemies before they hit you!
