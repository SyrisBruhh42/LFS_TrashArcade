HOW TO PLAY

You are a Cyber Ronin in the virtual Dojo.

Movement: WASD / Arrows / Left Joystick

Grapple/Slash: Click / Space / Right Button

Grapple onto anchor points to swing using orbital physics.

Collect energy nodes to grow your tether (tail). Longer tethers score higher combo multipliers, but if enemies hit your tail, you take damage!

Risk it all for the High Score.
