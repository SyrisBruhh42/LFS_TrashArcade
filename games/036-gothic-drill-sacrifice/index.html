HOW TO PLAY

Move/Drill: WASD or Arrow Keys (or Touch D-Pad)

Overdrive: Spacebar (or Touch OD button)

Drill through earth blocks to descend. Avoid indestructible bedrock.

Ghosts will drain your health if touched. Drill them while in OVERDRIVE to destroy them.

Activating OVERDRIVE costs 10% Health, but makes you invulnerable, faster, and destroys ghosts for massive points!

Collect green soul gems to restore health.
