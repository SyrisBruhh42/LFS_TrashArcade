How to Play

Move: WASD / Arrow Keys

Grapple: Click and drag from your robot to a ceiling scrap point to swing.

Echo Ghost: Press Space (or ECHO button) to record your movement. Press again to spawn a ghost that repeats it! Use the ghost to hit switches you can't reach.

Goal: Reach the exit portal in each junkyard sector.
