How to Play

Swing through the desert sandstorm using magnetic grapple points.

Controls:

Desktop: WASD/Arrows to swing, Space to jump.
Mouse Click to grapple, Right Click/Shift to toggle polarity.

Touch: On-screen joystick to swing. Tap right side to grapple/jump, double tap to toggle polarity.

Mechanics:

Blue nodes = North, Red nodes = South.
Opposite colors ATTRACT (pull in).
Same colors REPEL (push away).
