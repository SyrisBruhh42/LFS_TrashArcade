How to Play

Goal: Drill as deep as possible into the microscopic cell while managing your polarity.

Controls:
- Keyboard: WASD / Arrows to move. Space / Enter to switch polarity.
- Touch: Drag left side to move. Tap right side to switch polarity.

Mechanics:
- Polarity: You are either Positive (Red) or Negative (Blue).
- Like polarities repel each other. Opposite polarities attract.
- Attract resource nodes of the opposite polarity to gain points and health.
- Repel hazards of the same polarity to avoid damage.
