How to Play

Move/Aim: WASD / Arrows / Mouse

Fire: Space / Click. Firing pushes you backward! Use recoil to move!

Echo: Shift / Right Click. Creates a time-delayed ghost that repeats your actions.

Bounce around the candy conveyor, use your echoes to cross-fire at enemies, and survive!
