How to Play

You are stationed on the Candy Factory Conveyor Line. Rogue candies are attacking!

Movement: WASD / Arrows / D-Pad
Attack (Slash): Space / Click / Tap
Parry: Slash right before an attack hits you!

Mechanic: Slashes use Energy. When you slash, the energy crystal drops on the conveyor. You MUST physically walk over it to retrieve it! No energy = no slash.
