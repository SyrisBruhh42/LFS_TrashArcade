HOW TO PLAY

Goal: Survive the rhythm-synced hazards of the Arctic Base.

Mechanic: Collect Data Cores to grow your tail. A longer tail increases your score multiplier but makes it harder to dodge hazards.

Rhythm Sync: Move or press SYNC to the beat (background pulse). Perfect timing grants bonus points and brief invulnerability.

Desktop: WASD/Arrows to move. SPACE to Sync.
Touch: Virtual Joystick to move. Tap SYNC button.
