How to Play

Goal: Defend the core of the clockwork foundry against waves of rogue automatons.

Building: Click on empty nodes to build towers (costs 20 Scrap).

Polarity (The Twist): Your base has a global Polarity (Blue or Red). Press Spacebar or the touch button to switch it. Blue attracts enemies towards towers, Red repels them away from towers (but towards the base if you're not careful!). Towers deal double damage to enemies with the opposite polarity.
