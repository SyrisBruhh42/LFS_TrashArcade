HOW TO PLAY

- WASD/Arrows to move, Mouse to aim & shoot.

- Touch controls: Left side drag to move, right side tap to shoot.

- You have ONE projectile. Shoot to disable guards.

- You must physically walk over the projectile to retrieve it before you can shoot again!

- Avoid guard vision cones (red lines).

- Steal the objective (yellow) and reach the exit (blue) to clear the level.
