How to Play

Move: WASD / Arrows / Virtual Joystick (Left)

Shoot: Mouse Click+Drag / Touch Drag (Right)

Your balls (ammo) bounce off walls and bumpers. They damage enemies at high speed.

You only have 3 balls. You must physically walk over a stopped/slow ball to retrieve it to shoot again!
